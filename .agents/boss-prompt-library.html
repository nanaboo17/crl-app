📊 Daily Check-ins
Every morning — quick overview of today's performance
  • Show me today's numbers
  • What are today's KPIs?
  • Give me the daily summary
  • How did we perform yesterday?
  • Show me churn, gross adds, and sales for today
  • What's our active customer count right now?
🔄 Comparisons
Day-over-Day
  • Compare today's churn vs yesterday
  • How are gross adds today compared to yesterday?
  • Is sales up or down from yesterday?
Week-over-Week
  • Compare this week's performance to last week
  • How does this week's churn compare to last week?
  • Are we doing better or worse than last week?
Month-over-Month
  • Compare July vs June numbers
  • How is this month's gross adds compared to last month?
  • Show me month-over-month churn trend
Custom Periods
  • Compare Q2 vs Q1 performance
  • Show me the last 7 days vs the previous 7 days
  • How did we do this week vs same week last year?
📈 Trends & Patterns
  • Show me churn trend for the last 30 days
  • Is our customer base growing or shrinking?
  • What's the trend for gross adds this month?
  • Are sales trending up or down?
  • Show me the weekly trend for the last 3 months
🚨 Anomalies & Problems
  • Why did churn spike yesterday?
  • What's causing the drop in gross adds?
  • Any unusual changes in today's data?
  • Which region has the highest churn right now?
  • Are there any red flags I should know about?
  • Why is revenue down this week?
🔍 Drill-down & Segmentation
By Region
  • Break down churn by region
  • Which region has the best sales performance?
  • Show me gross adds by region for this week
By Product / Plan
  • Which product has the highest churn?
  • Break down sales by plan type
  • Show me gross adds by product
By Time
  • Which day this week had the highest churn?
  • What time of month do we get the most gross adds?
  • Show me daily breakdown for this month
By Customer Segment
  • What's the churn rate for prepaid vs postpaid?
  • Which customer segment is growing fastest?
  • Show me enterprise vs consumer numbers
🔮 Forecasting
  • What's the churn forecast for next month?
  • Predict gross adds for the next 2 weeks
  • Will we hit our sales target this month?
  • What's the projected customer count by end of quarter?
  • At this rate, where will churn be by December?
🎯 Targets & Performance
  • Are we on track to hit this month's target?
  • How far are we from our gross adds target?
  • What percentage of our sales target have we achieved?
  • How many more gross adds do we need to hit target?
  • Show me actual vs target for all KPIs
📋 Reports & Exports
  • Generate a weekly report
  • Give me the numbers I need for the Monday meeting
  • Create a summary for management
  • Export this month's data to sheets
  • Prepare the monthly performance review
💡 Strategic / Ad-hoc
  • What's our biggest problem right now?
  • Where should we focus to reduce churn?
  • Which region needs the most attention?
  • What would happen if churn stays at this rate?
  • What's driving our growth this month?
